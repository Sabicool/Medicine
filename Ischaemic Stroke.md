# Clinical Features
1. Divide the CN's up by 4's. They correspond to the sections of the brainstem as you increase in number by 4. For example: CN 1-4 (Midbrain), CN5-8 (Pons), CN9-12 (Medulla).
2. Then, CN's that divide equally into 12 are MIDLINE and those who do NOT divided equally into 12 are LATERAL. For example: CN 3, 4, 6, and 12 are all MIDLINE. CN 5, 7, 9, 10, and 11 are all LATERAL (you omit the first 2 CNs). 
3. Next, determine if the deficit is MOTOR or SENSORY. Motor deficits are midline and sensory deficits are lateral (sensory = side)
