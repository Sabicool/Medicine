- Absorption is the amount of intact drug which is absorbed from the gut into portal circulation ($f_{g}$ when expressed as a fraction)
- First-pass clearance is the extent to which a drug is removed by the liver during the first passage in the portal blood through the liver to enter systemic circulation
- Bioavailability refers to the fraction of the dose which reaches systemic circulation as intact drug ($F$)
$$
\text{Bioavailability }(F)=\text{Fraction Absorbed }(f_{g})\times \text{Fraction Escaping First Pass Clearance }(f_{H})
$$
