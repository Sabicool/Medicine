# Definitions
- Pharmacokinetics is what the body does to the drug
- Pharmocodynamics is what the drug does to the body
- It is convention in pharmacology to use plasma concentration
	- The relationship between the plasma concentration and blood concentration is given by the blood:plasma concentration ratio ($\lambda$):
$$
\begin{align}
\lambda &=\frac{\text{Drug concentration in whole blood}}{\text{Drug concentration in plasma}}=\frac{C_{b}}{C} \\
C_{b} & = \lambda \cdot C
\end{align}
$$
