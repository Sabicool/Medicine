# Physiology
- Contents of the abdomen are predominantly non-compressible fluid
- The rpessure increases with inspiration and decreases with expiration
- A normal person has either a negative intra-abdominal pressure, or something in the range of 0-5 mmHg
	- The morbidly obese and pregnant patient may have pressures as high as 10-15 mmHg
- In normal critically ill patients the pressure is 5-7 mmHg and the upper limit of normal is 12 mmHg
- Abdominal perfusion pressure is given by:
$$
\text{Abdominal Perfusion Pressure}=\text{MAP}-\text{Intra-Abdominal Pressure}
$$
- A target abdominal perfusion pressure ≥60 mmHg has been demonstrated to improve survival
