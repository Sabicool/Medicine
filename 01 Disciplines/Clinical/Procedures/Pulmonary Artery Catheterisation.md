# Contraindications
- Absolute
	- Right sided endocarditis
	- Right sided masses (e.g. myxoma) or thrombi
- Relative
	- Severe coagulopathy
	- Severe thrombocytopaenia
- Reasons for caution
	- Tricuspid regurgitation - difficult passage of catheter
	- Left bundle branch block - catheter passage may induce complete heart block ⇒ be prepared to place a transvenous pacemaker should this occur

# Complications
- Pulmonary artery perforation
	- Risk factors
		- Older age
		- Prolonged balloon inflation
		- Pulmonary hypertension
		- Anticoagulation
	- Clinical features
		- Haemoptysis
		- Hypoxaemia
		- Shock
	- Management
		- Inflate the balloon
		- Intubate the patient with a dual-lumen endotracheal tube
		- Lateral decubitus position with affected side down
		- Emergency consultation with cardiothoracic surgeon and interventional radiology
# Calculations
- Fick equation where:
	- $\text{CO}$ is the cardiac output (L) which is normally 4.8 - 7.3 L
	- $\dot{V}\ce{O2}$ is the oxygen consumption (mL/min)
	- $C_{\text{a}}$ is the oxygen content of arterial blood (mL/L)
	- $C_{\text{v}}$ is the oxygen content of venous blood (mL/L)
$$
\text{CO}=\frac{\dot{V}\ce{O2}}{C_{\text{a}}-C_{\text{v}}}=\frac{\dot{V}\ce{O2}}{1.36\times \text{Hb}\times (S_\text{a}\ce{O2}-S_\text{v}\ce{O2}) \times 10}
$$
- Cardiac index equation where:
	- $\text{CI}$ is the cardiac index (L/min m^2) which is normally 2.8 - 4.2 L
	- $\text{BSA}$ is the body surface area (m^2)
$$
\text{CI}=\frac{\text{CO}}{\text{BSA}}
$$
- Systemic vascular resistance where:
	- $\text{SVR}$ is the systemic vascular resistance (dyn sec/cm^5) which is normally 700 - 1600
	- $\text{MAP}$ is the mean arterial pressure (mmHg)
	- $\text{RA}$ is the right atrial mean pressure (mmHg)
	- $\text{CO}$ is the cardiac output (L)
$$
\text{SVR}=\frac{\text{MAP}-\text{RA}}{\text{CO}}\times 80
$$
- Pulmonary vascular resistance where:
	- $\text{PVR}$ is the pulmonary vascular resistance (dyn sec/cm^5) which is normally 20 - 130
	- $\text{PA}$ is the pulmonary artery mean pressure  (mmHg)
	- $\text{PCWP}$ is the pulmonary capillary wedge pressure (mmHg)
	- $\text{CO}$ is the cardiac output (L)
$$
\text{PVR}=\frac{\text{PA}-\text{PCWP}}{\text{CO}}\times 80
$$
