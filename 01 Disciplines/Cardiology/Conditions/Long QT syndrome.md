# Aetiologies
- Congenital (LQT1,2,3)
- Acquired
	- Electrolyte abnormalities: [[Hypokalaemia]], [[Hypomagnesaemia]]
	- [[Hypothermia]]
	- Drugs
		- Class 1 and III anti-arrhythmics
		- Antimocribals (erythromycin)
		- Tricyclics
	- Intracranial bleeding
	- Heart-block with bradycardia
