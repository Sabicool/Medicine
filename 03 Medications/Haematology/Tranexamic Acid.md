- Antifibrinolytic: lysine analogue that blocks plasminogen's lysine binding sites, so fibrin isn't broken down
- Used in trauma, orthopaedics and surgery with significant oozing or blood loss
- Timing and dose in trauma: [gap]
- High doses can cause seizures

> [!card]- How does tranexamic acid work?
> A lysine analogue that blocks the lysine binding sites on plasminogen, so plasmin can't break down fibrin.
