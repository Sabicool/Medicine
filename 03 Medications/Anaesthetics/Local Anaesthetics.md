# Maximum Doses

| Drug        | Plain    | With adrenaline |
| ----------- | -------- | --------------- |
| Lignocaine  | 3 mg/kg  | 7 mg/kg         |
| Bupivacaine | 2 mg/kg  | 2 mg/kg         |
| Ropivacaine | 3 mg/kg  | 3 mg/kg         |
| Prilocaine  | 6 mg/kg  | 8 mg/kg         |

- Dose on lean body weight, since toxicity comes from the peak plasma level
- Doses of different local anaesthetics add up
- Use less in the elderly and in hepatic or cardiac impairment
- Strength in mg/mL is the % × 10 (e.g. 1% = 10 mg/mL)
