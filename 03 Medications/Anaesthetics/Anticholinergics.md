- Antimuscarinics, so block vagal slowing of the heart and ↑ HR

# Atropine
- Emergency dose for profound bradycardia: 600 mcg IV
- Larger, faster rise in HR than glycopyrrolate
- Tertiary amine so crosses the BBB, causing confusion especially in the elderly
- Small doses can cause a paradoxical bradycardia

# Glycopyrrolate
- Also called glycopyrronium
- Dose: 200 mcg IV
- Milder rise in HR
- Quaternary ammonium so doesn't cross the BBB, also dries secretions
- Given with neostigmine, see [[Acetylcholinesterase Inhibitor]]

> [!card]- Why doesn't glycopyrrolate cause confusion like atropine?
> It's a quaternary ammonium, so it doesn't cross the blood–brain barrier.

> [!cloze]
> The atropine dose for profound bradycardia is ==600 mcg== IV.
