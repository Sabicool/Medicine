- For analgesia:
	- 0.25 mg/kg IV or 5-10 mg IV boluses titrated to effect
- For procedural sedation
	- 0.5-1mg/kg IV
	- Consider adding ondansetron for dissociative procedural sedation in whom emesis could be harmful (e.g. spinal precautions, traumatic brain injury, ocular injury, penetrating abdominal injury)
- For delayed sequence induction
	- 1mg/kg IV or 1-3mg/kg IM

> [!NOTE] Dose reduce with comorbidities
> Dose reduce for:
> - Patients with shock significant haemodynamic compromise
> - Hepatic impairment
> - Frailty or advanced age
